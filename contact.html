Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. Kitchen and bathroom renovations typically take 4-8 weeks, while custom home builds can take 6-12 months. We'll provide a detailed timeline during your consultation.

Do you offer free consultations?

Yes! We offer complimentary initial consultations to discuss your project, understand your vision, and provide preliminary recommendations. Contact us to schedule yours today.

Are you licensed and insured?

Absolutely. A Fab Home is fully licensed, bonded, and insured. We maintain all required permits and insurance coverage to protect our clients and our team.

What is your service area?

We primarily serve the greater metro area and surrounding communities within a 50-mile radius. For projects outside this area, please contact us to discuss possibilities.
